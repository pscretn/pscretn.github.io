Privacy Policy

WaveLink DMX

Effective Date: October 28, 2026

1. Introduction

WaveLink DMX is a professional DMX lighting controller application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle user and device data.

2. Data Collection

WaveLink DMX does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app does not require user accounts, login details, or personal identification.

3. Network Usage

The app sends DMX control data to your lighting equipment on your local network or over USB: the WaveLink DMX controller, Art-Net nodes, or a USB DMX interface. This traffic stays on your local network or cable.

When you check for or install a controller firmware update, the app downloads the firmware from our public release page on GitHub. No personal data is sent; as with any download, GitHub receives the device's network address. No other data is sent to external servers.

4. Audio (Live Audio)

The optional Live Audio feature runs the light show in time with the music playing on your device. To hear that music, Android requires the app to use audio playback capture, which needs the record audio (microphone) permission and your confirmation in Android's own "Start recording or casting" prompt each time Live Audio starts.

  • Only the audio playing on the device is used. The microphone is not used to listen to your surroundings, and the screen is never captured.
  • The audio is analysed on the device, as it plays, to find its tempo, beat and loudness. It is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.
  • Capture runs only while Live Audio is on, and a notification is shown for as long as it runs. It stops when you turn Live Audio off or stop sharing from Android.

5. Permissions

  • Record audio: for Live Audio, as described above.
  • Notifications: to show that Live Audio or background lighting output is running.
  • Network and Wi-Fi: to talk to your lighting equipment and download controller firmware.
  • Foreground services: to keep lighting output and Live Audio running while the screen is off.
  • USB: to drive a WaveLink controller or USB DMX interface that you plug in.

6. Local Storage

The app stores the following data locally on the user's device:

  • Saved scenes
  • Bank configurations
  • Fixture personalities and patched fixtures
  • Channel shortcuts
  • Effects, light show and output settings
  • Controller firmware you have downloaded

This data remains on the device and is not shared with third parties.

7. Third-Party Services

WaveLink DMX does not use analytics services, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking technologies.

8. Children's Privacy

WaveLink DMX is not designed for children under the age of 13. The app does not knowingly collect data from children.
