Privacy Policy

WaveLink DMX

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Introduction

WaveLink DMX is a professional DMX lighting controller application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle user and device data.

2. Data Collection

WaveLink DMX does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app does not require user accounts, login details, or personal identification.

3. Network Usage

The app communicates locally with an ESP32 device over Wi-Fi to send DMX control data. No data is transmitted to external servers.

4. Local Storage

The app stores the following data locally on the user's device:

  • Saved scenes
  • Bank configurations
  • Fixture personalities
  • Channel shortcuts

This data remains on the device and is not shared with third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

WaveLink DMX does not use analytics services, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking technologies.

6. Children's Privacy

WaveLink DMX is not designed for children under the age of 13. The app does not knowingly collect data from children.
