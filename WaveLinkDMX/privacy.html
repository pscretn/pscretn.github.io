Privacy Policy – WaveLink DMX WaveLink DMX does not collect, store, or share any personal user data. The app communicates locally with an ESP32 device over Wi-Fi to send DMX control data. No data is transmitted to external servers. The app may store the following data locally on the user’s device: Saved scenes Bank configurations Fixture personalities Channel shortcuts This data is stored only on the device and is not shared with third parties.
