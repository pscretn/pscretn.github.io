A little history

Sweetness, the monks' way.

Monk fruit is a small green melon native to Southeast Asia. Buddhist monks prized it for its natural sweetness and therapeutic benefits, which is how it got its name.

We extract its pure sweetness, nearly 200 times sweeter than sugar, and blend it with natural fruit-based erythritol so it measures 1:1 with sugar. No chemical additives. No lab-made flavours.
