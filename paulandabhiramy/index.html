Two years.

I keep trying to find the big words for it, and they don't come. What comes instead are small things. The way you say my name when you're half asleep. The sound of you laughing at something before you've even finished telling me what it was. The fact that I've stopped counting how many times you've made an ordinary Tuesday feel like something worth remembering.

I think that's the secret nobody tells you about love. It isn't the grand moments. It's that somewhere along the way you became the person I want to tell things to first — the good news, the bad news, the stupid thought I had at two in the morning. You became my default.

Two years ago I didn't know what we'd become. Today I know, and I still want more of it.

Thank you for choosing me on the easy days and on the hard ones. Thank you for being loud in my quiet life.

Happy anniversary, my love. Here's to the next two, and the two after that, and every one that follows.

All my heart,Paul
