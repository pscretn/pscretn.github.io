06Questions

Which Resolume and which computers?

Resolume Arena 7 on Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. The plugins are FFGL plugins and run on the graphics card. There is no Mac version yet.

Do I need the internet at a show?

No. The licence is checked on your computer, offline, every time. Buying and receiving the licence happen on WhatsApp.

I changed computers, or replaced the motherboard.

The licence belongs to one computer. Send us your new Machine ID and your licence ID (both shown in the License Manager) on WhatsApp, and we reissue your licence.

I pasted the licence but still see the watermark.

Restart Resolume: it reads licences when it starts. If the License Manager says the licence is for another machine, it was made for a different Machine ID; send us the ID it shows.

The audio controls do nothing.

Turn Modulation up from 0: it is off by default, so plugins look the same until you use it. LFO and Tempo work without audio; Bass, Mid, Treble, Level and Beat need audio playing in Resolume.

Where do the plugins install?

Into Documents\Resolume Arena\Extra Effects\TrilitLabs FX, where Resolume finds them by itself. Updating or uninstalling keeps your licence.
