About

I read the floor, then I play it.

I'm Paul Sabu — the same hands behind pscretn's tech work, on the decks on weekends. The job is simple to describe and hard to do: watch who's actually dancing, work out what they want next, and get there without breaking the energy.

No fixed setlist. Malayalam and Tamil floor-fillers, Hindi and Punjabi when the crowd leans that way, commercial and EDM when the night needs lifting. A wedding reception and a college fest are not the same room, and they don't get the same set.
