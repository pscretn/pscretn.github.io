Privacy Policy

Trilit Mapper · Effective Date: November 7, 2026

Trilit Mapper is a projection mapping app for Android made by TrilitLabs. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: we do not collect your personal data, and nothing you create in the app is sent to us.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not send data to TrilitLabs or to any server we run.

2. Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only if you turn on music sync. The sound is analysed live on your device to measure level, frequency bands and beats, so that visuals can move with the music. Audio is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere. You can turn this off in the app, or remove the permission in your device settings, at any time.

3. Camera, Photos, Images and Video

If you choose to take a photo, the app opens your device's camera app. If you choose an image or video, you pick it with your device's file picker. These files are used on your device to show them on a surface or to find the outline of an object. They are not uploaded.

4. Your Shows and Projects

Your shows are saved in the app's private storage on your device. A show file leaves your device only if you choose to export or share it. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your personal device backup, which is managed by your own Google account and which we cannot access.

5. Local Network and Art-Net

The app can send and receive Art-Net lighting data if you turn this on. This lighting control data goes between your device and the equipment or address you set up, normally on your own local network. It is not sent to TrilitLabs. The app also uses network state to find your network addresses, and second screens or wireless displays are handled by your device's operating system.

6. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, share or transfer your information to any third party, because we do not collect it.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Everything stays on your device. To delete it, delete your shows in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Files you exported yourself are not removed by uninstalling.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
